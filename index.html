How It All Began

Our Story

London, 2022

It all began in London in 2022. One chance meeting turned into endless conversations, and before long we knew we'd found our person.

And then there were three

It wasn't long before our little family grew by four paws. Birley, our cocker spaniel, has been with us through every move — and naturally, will be part of the celebrations, bow tie and all. 🐾

Always on the move

Settling down has never really been our style. We've packed up and started somewhere new roughly every six months, collecting a wonderful, ever-growing circle of friends from all corners of the world along the way.

The world & the question

Together we've travelled the globe, chasing new places, new food and new adventures. Last year, somewhere along the journey, the question was finally popped — and the answer, of course, was a joyful "yes". Now we can't wait to bring all those friends together in Mallorca.

Getting There

Travel & Stay

Mallorca is easier to reach than you'd think — here's everything you need to plan the trip.

Fly In

Fly into Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI), which has direct flights from most major European cities. From the airport it's about a 40-minute drive to the venue.

Where to Stay

We're putting together a list of recommended hotels and a group room block near the venue. Details coming soon — for now, June is peak season, so we'd suggest booking your travel early.

Getting Around

We recommend renting a car to explore the island. A shuttle will run between the recommended hotels and the venue on the wedding day so you can relax and enjoy the cava.

Make a Holiday of It

Explore Mallorca

You've come all this way — stay a few extra days! Here are some of our favourite corners of the island.

Palma & the Cathedral

Wander the old town and see La Seu, the stunning seafront Gothic cathedral. Great for tapas, boutiques and people-watching.

Sóller & the Train

Ride the vintage wooden train through the mountains to Sóller, then a tram down to the harbour for lunch by the sea.

Deià & Valldemossa

Two of the prettiest villages in the Serra de Tramuntana (a UNESCO site) — stone houses, art, and dramatic coastal views.

Beaches & Coves

Swim in turquoise calas like Cala Llombards and Es Trenc, or hire a boat for the day along the coast.

Food & Wine

Try pa amb oli, fresh seafood and an ensaïmada pastry, and taste local wines in the Binissalem region.

Mountains & Hikes

Hike or drive the Tramuntana mountains, visit Cap de Formentor, and catch a Mediterranean sunset to remember.

FAQ

What should I wear?

Think Mediterranean summer — semi-formal / cocktail attire in light, breathable fabrics. June in Mallorca is warm and the ceremony is outdoors on grass and stone, so flat or block heels are your friend.

Is it really a two-day celebration?

Yes! Join us for welcome drinks and dinner on Friday the 18th, and the wedding itself on Saturday the 19th. We'd love for you to be there for both.

Will there be a special guest of honour?

Of course — Birley, our cocker spaniel, will be there in spirit (and quite possibly a bow tie). Keep an eye out for the fluffiest member of the wedding party. 🐾

Can I bring a plus-one?

We've reserved seats for the guests named on your invitation. If your invitation includes a guest, you'll be able to add them when you RSVP.

What about dietary restrictions?

Absolutely — there's a field in the RSVP form where you can let us know about any allergies or dietary needs.
