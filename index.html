FAQ

What should I wear?

The dress code is semi-formal / cocktail attire. The ceremony is outdoors on grass, so you may want to skip the stilettos!

Can I bring a plus-one?

We've reserved seats for the guests named on your invitation. If your invitation includes a guest, you'll be able to add them when you RSVP.

Are kids welcome?

We love your little ones, but this will be an adults-only celebration so everyone can relax and enjoy the evening.

What about dietary restrictions?

Absolutely — there's a field in the RSVP form where you can let us know about any allergies or dietary needs.
